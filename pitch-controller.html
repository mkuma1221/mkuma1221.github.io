Pitch Controller Model

Project Overview and Goals

  • Developed a closed-loop pitch autopilot for a fourth-order longitudinal aircraft state-space model in MATLAB and Simulink using true airspeed, angle of attack, pitch angle, and pitch rate.
  • Verified open-loop stability, controllability, and observability through eigenvalue analysis and MATLAB rank tests.
  • Implemented a PID controller with an interactive Simulink tuning dashboard and realistic actuator dynamics, including elevator saturation (±25°), rate limits (±30°/s), and first-order actuator lag.
  • Improved robustness by integrating a Kalman filter with Gaussian sensor and aerodynamic disturbance noise, and added anti-windup compensation to reduce saturation-induced overshoot.
  • Validated performance using 10° step, 10° doublet, and 15° square-wave commands, with real-time visualization through FlightGear and an artificial horizon display.
